About CARE

Care, Aid & Rehabilitation in Emergency

CARE is a non-profit organization committed to improving access to essential medicines and healthcare for underserved communities across Pakistan — driven by student leadership, medical partnerships and compassionate action.

We help bridge gaps in care by supporting access to medicines, promoting preventive health, and working alongside hospitals and community partners to improve health outcomes.

Our Programs

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide immediate, compassionate medical care and patient support to underserved communities, regardless of financial status.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where every person has access to dignified, quality healthcare in times of crisis and beyond.

Our Values

Compassion, Transparency, Integrity, Community — the four pillars that guide every decision and action we take.

Our Story

Founded to make healthcare more accessible — and growing ever since.

CARE began with a simple conviction: that no one should go without essential care because of cost or circumstance. From a small student-led effort, we have grown into an organization running a Drug Bank, medical camps and community outreach.

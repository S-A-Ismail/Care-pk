Why CARE Exists

Access to healthcare is influenced by more than the availability of hospitals and clinics. For many individuals and families, the cost of medicines, limited health awareness, and barriers to ongoing care can make treatment difficult to complete.

CARE was established to help bridge these gaps by supporting access to essential medicines, promoting preventive health, and working alongside healthcare institutions and community partners to improve health outcomes.

Through collaboration, volunteer engagement, and responsible stewardship of resources, we strive to make healthcare more accessible for those who need it most.

What We Do

Comprehensive support for a healthier tomorrow

Essential Medicines & Drug Bank

Supporting access to prescribed medicines through our Drug Bank for patients who cannot afford them.

Medical Outreach & Health Camps

Free medical camps and outreach that bring consultations, diagnostics and medicines to underserved communities.

Health Education & Prevention

Preventive health awareness sessions that help communities make informed decisions before problems become serious.

Patient Support Services

Helping patients continue and complete treatment through practical and financial support.

Volunteer & Student Leadership

Student-led volunteer initiatives that power our programs and develop the next generation of changemakers.

Emergency Assistance

Timely support for patients and families facing urgent medical need.
